Between [HEATCALLER OPERATOR NAME - CHECK] ("we") and [CLUB NAME] ("the club").

## 1. What this is

The club is testing HeatCaller, race timing and announcing software, before it is generally released. It is **beta software**: it has been tested at solo rehearsals but not yet at many club nights, and it will have faults.

The announcer is least accurate on short tracks. Where laps average under 8 seconds, spoken calls and commentary can come late, be skipped, or give a lap time, gap or place that is wrong or out of date. This does not affect lap timing or scoring.

# Intellectual property assignment

**This assignment** is made on ______________ 2026 (the "Effective Date")

**by** Asher Kent, of 506 Pinyon Dr, Schenectady, NY 12303 (the "Founder"),

**to** HeatCaller LLC, a New York limited liability company (DOS ID 8031449), of 506 Pinyon Dr, Schenectady, NY 12303 (the "Company").

## 1. Background

The Founder created the HeatCaller software and related materials, some before the Company was formed on 26 September 2026 and some since. The Founder and the Company want all of it to belong to the Company. This assignment confirms that it does, and transfers anything that does not already.

## 2. What is assigned

"**HeatCaller IP**" means everything the Founder has made, alone or with others, for or relating to HeatCaller, at any time before or after the Effective Date, in any form, and all rights in it anywhere in the world, including:

- **Code:** the source and object code of the HeatCaller desktop app, the relay, the website, tools and scripts, tests, and everything in the HeatCaller repositories (on 16 Nov 2026: github.com/heatcaller/HeatCaller, heatcaller/HeatCaller-releases, heatcaller/heatcaller-site and heatcaller/HeatCaller-Admin. The heatcaller/SmartThingsPublic fork is other people's code and is not included);
- **Assets:** audio (music, sound packs, show music, imaging and recorded or generated voice lines), images, logos, icons, video, fonts the Founder made, and the data files that ship with the software;
- **Writing:** documentation, the website, plans, designs, mock-ups and marketing material;
- **Names and marks:** the names "HeatCaller" and any other product or feature names, logos and slogans, with the goodwill of the business they represent, and any applications to register them;
- **Domains and accounts:** the domain heatcaller.com and any other domains, and the accounts used to run HeatCaller (code hosting, app hosting, email, the voice and AI providers, payment and app-store accounts), as far as they can be transferred (on 16 Nov 2026: the heatcaller GitHub organization, the Fly.io app heatcaller-relay and its volume, the Cloudflare account holding R2 video, the ElevenLabs and Anthropic API accounts, the Porkbun account that registers heatcaller.com, and the Google (Gmail) account used to send and receive the heatcaller.com email. There are no payment or app-store accounts yet);
- **Know-how:** inventions, ideas, methods, decoder-protocol notes, and trade secrets; and
- **AI-assisted work:** anything above made with the help of AI tools (including code, text, audio and images generated or edited with them), to the full extent the Founder has any rights in it.

It includes all copyrights, trademark and trade-name rights, rights in inventions and any patents or applications, trade secret rights, database rights and moral rights (where they can be assigned), and the right to sue for past infringement.

## 3. Assignment

The Founder assigns to the Company all of the Founder's right, title and interest in the HeatCaller IP. As to HeatCaller IP that will exist in future, this is an assignment of the future rights when they arise. Where any right cannot be assigned, the Founder grants the Company an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence of it, with the right to sublicense, and waives (or agrees not to assert) any moral rights in it.

## 4. Payment

The Founder makes this assignment as part of the Founder's capital contribution to the Company, together with the Founder's initial cash contribution of US$500 deposited in the Company's bank account, in exchange for the Founder's membership interest in the Company, the receipt and sufficiency of which the Founder acknowledges.

## 5. What the Founder confirms

The Founder confirms that, to the Founder's knowledge:

1. the Founder made the HeatCaller IP or has the right to assign it;
2. the Founder has not assigned or licensed it to anyone else, other than open-source licences of third-party parts that HeatCaller uses under their own terms;
3. it was not made in the course of employment or under a contract that gives anyone else rights in it; and
4. where AI tools were used, the Founder used them under terms that do not give the tool's provider ownership of the output [CHECK: the terms of each tool used, e.g. Anthropic's for Claude, ElevenLabs' for generated audio].

The Founder makes no promise that AI-generated parts are protected by copyright. The Company accepts that some may not be.

## 6. Further help

The Founder will sign any further documents, and do anything else reasonably needed, to record or protect the Company's ownership, including transferring domains and accounts and signing filings with trademark and copyright offices. The Company pays the reasonable cost.

## 7. Third-party materials

This assignment does not cover materials the Founder did not make (open-source libraries, voice models, fonts and other parts listed in the software's **About HeatCaller** box). The Company uses those under their own licences.

## 8. General

This is the whole agreement about its subject. It is governed by the laws of the State of New York. If any part is unenforceable, the rest still applies.

# Questions for ElevenLabs (business / OEM terms)

From the roadmap's "The HeatCaller voice (ElevenLabs), as a business" section and the project's notes on managed voices.

1. **Reselling.** Do your business or OEM terms allow us to provide speech to clubs through our own service (our relay calls your API with our key and serves the audio to the club), and which plan is required?
2. **Caching and reuse.** May we keep rendered audio and replay it - shared between clubs for a line with no name in it, and per club for a line with a driver's name - and for how long?
3. **Which voices.** Which library voices may be used this way, commercially, through a reseller? Do voice owners' terms differ?
4. **End-user terms.** What terms must the clubs (our end users) agree to, and do you need them passed through word for word?
5. **Children's names.** Lines sent to you can contain children's first names and initials. What are your data retention, processing location and training terms for API input text and output audio, and is there a zero-retention option?
6. **Cloned voices.** If a club clones its own announcer's voice, what consent do you require, and who holds it?
7. **Attribution.** Any requirement to name ElevenLabs on the club's pages or in the app?
8. **Limits.** Rate and concurrency limits for a service with several clubs racing on the same evening.

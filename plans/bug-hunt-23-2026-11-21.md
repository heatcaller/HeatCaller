## Medium

| # | Where | Bug |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ProgramNextStep.cs:386` | Once qualifying is over and one class's finals are built, any other class with a recorded qualifier and no mains takes over the next step with "Qualifying is complete", seed mains (step 6a, added 20 Nov). If that class can't be seeded (its heat was recorded with no results, so seeding refuses with "No qualifying results") or the director doesn't want finals for it, the step never clears: Race Control's Start Next is the seed button, Auto start between races closes, and the Today card loses Start, Call and Skip for every remaining main. Before 6a this only showed once everything had run. |
| 2 | `TodayControl.cs:112`, `MainForm.Pages.cs:1887` | After a heat is run as a demo with "Let a demo fill in the race program" off, its card says "Demos don't move the night on" until the app is closed or another demo runs: the round is never forgotten, and that strip is chosen before the timer is looked at. A heat demoed as a sound check then gets no "Timer not connected. Laps won't be counted." when it is raced for real. |

## The fixes

| # | Fix |
|---|---|
| 1 | Step 6a only fires until the first final has run. A final started from the program says the director has gone on; the class is offered again at step 7, once everything has run. |
| 2 | With the timer down, the demo strip says "Timer not connected." first, then the demo's words. The demoed heat is forgotten as soon as a real race is staged. |
| 3 | The call says the round's classes as the announcer says them (`MixedHeats.SpokenClasses`): "Stock and Mod, Heat 4 drivers". |
| 4 | A night with a first race time has begun when that race is due, not at 06:00; one with no time begins at 06:00 as before. A first race before 06:00 is taken as after midnight. |

# HeatCaller bug hunt 21: 15 Nov 2026, code from 12 to 15 Nov (Fable 5.2)

Scope: source merged since 12 Nov, `git diff 7b465e76 bf312c8b`, about 60,000 added source lines, tests excluded. 11 Fable 5.2 hunters each read one slice plus the code around it, and tried to refute their own findings before reporting. Each was told to skip what bug hunts 19 and 20 already fixed. Nothing was built or run.

Findings: 35 (32 confirmed, 3 plausible). 9 medium, 26 low. None high. The three most serious were re-checked by hand against the code (marked ✔).

**Status: all 35 fixed** on branch `claude/bug-hunt-21` by 8 fixers, each in its own worktree, each with regression tests (`BugHunt21_*Tests.cs`, `relay/test/bughunt21-pages-push.test.ts`). Three loose ends the fixes left were then closed: "spick and span" no longer reads as a slur, a season at a club that never closes its race days still finishes once the last night is over, and the "first night" badge shows only on drivers who are checked in. Full laptop suite: 8,753 passed; the 4 failures were already on main and #219 fixed them. Relay: 1,976 passed.

Changes in behaviour worth knowing: the announcer's countdown and the night plan now count to the green, as the screens do (#22); Head to Head's grid, score and reading leave practice out (#33); "first night" now means no races before tonight, at a club with at least four nights on file (#9); a payment can now be split between cash and card (#12).

Coverage gaps: the app-shell hunter skipped many small files (DayScheduleControl, ScreenWords, DriverLive, BigScreenForm, PhotoFinishForm, CastControl and others under 30 changed lines). The relay hunter only skimmed the OBS overlay and photo-finish parts of `WebServerService*.cs`. The Track Designer piece code was read but not traced edit by edit. Timing/threading (warm-up flag, clock restore, removed laps, decoder reader, fold slide) and relay auth/tenancy were traced and came up clean.

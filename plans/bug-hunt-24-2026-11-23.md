# HeatCaller bug hunt 24: 23 Nov 2026, the whole codebase (Fable 5.2 swarm)

Scope: every shipped source file on main at `2d64ec0a` - the app, the timing layer and the relay, 883 files and about 565,000 lines, tests excluded. This is the first hunt over the whole codebase rather than what changed since the last one.

How it was run: 182 readers each read one shard of about 3,800 lines in full (partial-class families kept together; the four files over 6,000 lines split by line range with overlap), and 16 more read across files on one theme each (the app-to-relay payload, cross-thread access, the timing engine, the 6am night boundary, saving and loading, relay sign-in, relay escaping, stale calls, race flow, the phone pages and service worker, the local web server, privacy, long-night leaks, audio and voice, import and export, numbers and culture). Each reader had to try to refute a suspect against the code, the WHY comments, the tests and the earlier hunt reports before writing it down. That gave 397 raw findings, 383 after merging reports of the same spot. Then 475 sceptics each tried to knock one down by reading the code again: two per high or medium finding (one tracing the code path, one asking whether it is deliberate, tested or already fixed), one per low. Nothing was built or run during the hunt.

Findings: 368 confirmed by every sceptic that read them. 2 high, 62 medium, 304 low. The severity shown is the lower of the sceptics' ratings. 12 were knocked down and 3 split the sceptics; both are listed at the end and are not fixed.

**Status: fixes in progress on `claude/bug-hunt-24`.**

Coverage gaps: nothing was run, so every finding is confirmed by reading only. The readers noted 748 places where they stopped tracing, mostly a caller or a reader in another shard that they did not follow to the end. Low findings had one sceptic, not two. Tests, the website docs and the audio assets were not read.

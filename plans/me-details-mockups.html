Change your details: two ways

These mockups go with plans/me-details-redesign-plan.md. A gives each group its own short sheet, opened from the card. B keeps one button and one sheet, with tabs across the top. Both use the same card rows, fields, locked rows, switches and crew cards, so they differ only in how you get in and how Send works. Each sheet scrolls inside its phone, and its Send stays pinned at the bottom.

A. Four sheets, opened from the card

Each group on the card has its own Change ›. Every sheet does one job, has one Send and fits on about a screen.

B. One sheet with tabs

The card keeps one button. The sheet has four tabs across the top, and typing on one tab is kept when you switch to another. A dot marks each tab with unsent changes. One Send sends everything changed on every tab: one request for details and one for crew.

# heatcaller.com: testing with race directors (Phase 8)

20 Nov 2026. Phase 8 of [website-improvement-plan.md](website-improvement-plan.md). It runs before any decision on splitting the site into pages (Phase 7.1). About 20 minutes per director. Three to five directors is enough to find the big problems.

## Who

Race directors, or whoever runs timing, at clubs that **don't** know HeatCaller yet. Mix them: one who uses LiveTime, one on paper or a basic lap counter, and one under 30 who'll open it on a phone. Not friends who've seen the app.

## Set-up

- Their own phone first, then a laptop if there's time. Most visits will come from phones.
- Send them `https://heatcaller.com/?utm_source=director-test`, so their visits can be left out of the real numbers.
- Ask them to think out loud. You don't explain, defend or point anything out. Note where they look, what they skip and where they get stuck.

## 1. Five-second test (before they scroll)

Show the first screen for five seconds, then hide it.

1. What is it?
2. Who's it for?
3. What would you do next?

**Pass:** they say "race timing" and "announcer" (or "commentary"), and "for clubs". **Watch for:** "a voice app", "a video thing", or no idea who it's for.

## 2. Tasks

Give one at a time. Don't help unless they're stuck for over a minute, and write down where.

| # | Task | Answer on the page | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Does it work with your decoder?" | Hero chips, What you need, the FAQ | Time to find it. Did they open the FAQ? |
| 2 | "What does it cost?" | Nowhere yet (plan item 3.4) | How they react to no price: shrug, worry, or leave? |
| 3 | "What happens if the laptop crashes mid-race?" | Race night, Switching over | Did they believe it? |
| 4 | "How would your drivers know when they're racing?" | Race night (drivers), Online, /drivers | Did they find the phone screens? |
| 5 | "How would you move your club over from what you use now?" | Switching over | Was anything missing for their setup? |
| 6 | "Sign up for the beta." Stop them at the form. | Join the beta (header, hero, Join bar on phones, the beta box) | Which button they used. Did the "five-minute form" put them off? |

## 3. Questions after

1. What would stop you trying it at your club?
2. What would you need to see or hear to switch?
3. Was anything confusing or hard to believe?
4. Who else at the club would need to say yes, and what would they ask?
5. What would you tell another director it is, in one sentence?

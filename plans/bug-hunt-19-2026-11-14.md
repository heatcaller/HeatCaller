### 1. A driver's "video starts in the wrong place" or "hide this video" request from their phone is silently thrown away and never reaches the desk.

`HeatCaller/HeatCaller/DriverAskContracts.cs:178` · high · confirmed

**What happens.** After a race with video, a driver taps "Video in the wrong place" or "Ask the club to hide this video" on their phone. The relay accepts it (relay/src/driverasks.ts:159-161 lists "videooff" and "videohide" in DRIVER_ASK_KINDS, and line 283 validates them). On the next inbox poll the laptop's ParseInbox runs `DriverAsk.IsKind(kind)`. That check does not include VideoOff or VideoHide, so the request is put in `unusable`, confirmed to the relay and dropped. It never shows in Driver Requests. The Noted/Approve paths (NoteVideoOff, ApproveVideoHide, the DriverRequestsForm cases, the summary lines) can never run, and the video is never hidden. The driver's phone also never shows an answer for it, because DriverLive drops any answer whose kind fails IsKind.

**Code path.** DriverAskContracts.cs: `public static bool IsKind(string? kind) => kind is Details or Pack or Query or Setup or PackEdit or Car or Photo or Late or Skip or Coming or Post or Swap or Cover;`. VideoOff and VideoHide are missing, although this same diff added both constants ("videooff", "videohide") to the file. DriverRequests.cs:692-697 calls `if (!DriverNight.IsADriverId(badge) || !DriverAsk.IsKind(kind) ...) { unusable.Add(id); continue; }`. RelayPublisher.Requests.cs:233 adds `unusable` to the ids confirmed back to the relay, which deletes its copy. DriverLive.cs:594 skips answers with `!DriverAsk.IsKind(answer.Kind)`. The handlers that cannot be reached: DriverRequests.cs:1823 (NoteVideoOff), DriverRequests.cs:1838 (ApproveVideoHide), DriverRequestsForm.cs:339-353, and MainForm.Video.cs:31, which wires HideRaceVideo.

**Verifier.** DriverAskContracts.cs:178 IsKind lists Details..Cover but omits VideoOff/VideoHide, which are defined at lines 44/47. ParseInbox (DriverRequests.cs:692) sends any kind that fails IsKind to `unusable`, which gets confirmed back to the relay and dropped. The relay accepts and queues both kinds (driverasks.ts:161, 283; pages.ts:2158/2164 send them). So every video ask is thrown away before it reaches the desk, and DriverLive.cs:594 also drops answers for these kinds. No other code path files these kinds. The desk tests (RaceVideoDeskTests) file the asks straight into the store and skip ParseInbox, so they don't catch this.

**Fix** (`2bae16a`). Confirmed: DriverAsk.IsKind (DriverAskContracts.cs:178) left out VideoOff and VideoHide, so ParseInbox confirmed every video ask to the relay as unusable and dropped it, and DriverLive skipped the desk's answers to them. I added both kinds to IsKind and corrected its out-of-date "four kinds" comment. IsWrite is unchanged, so both asks still wait for the desk. Test: BugHunt19_VideoAsksTests.Video_asks_from_the_inbox_reach_the_desk, BugHunt19_VideoAsksTests.The_desks_answer_to_a_video_ask_goes_back_to_the_phone.

### 2. Ticking or unticking a class in the Class cell saves an out-of-date copy of the driver, wiping a phone change made since the list was drawn.

`HeatCaller/HeatCaller/CheckInControl.Classes.cs:133` · medium · confirmed

**What happens.** A driver picks their car on the phone, or the desk approves the driver's own profile edits from their phone. Who's Racing is not redrawn. The desk then clicks the driver's Class cell and ticks Mod. ChangeClass edits the row's old DriverProfile object and upserts it, so the store goes back to the car and other fields from before the phone change.

**Code path.** CheckInControl.Classes.cs:92-113: PickClasses takes the profile from _grid.Rows[rowIndex].Tag, and the menu item's closure captures it. Lines 127-133: ChangeClass calls SetRunsClass(profile, ...) and then _profiles.Upsert(profile) without re-reading _profiles.Get or merging through ProfileStore.KeepChangesMadeSince. Phone paths replace the stored object (DriverRequests.Garage.cs:631 _profiles.Upsert(after) on a snapshot). The grid is only rebuilt by RefreshDriverList, and no profile-change event calls it.

**Verifier.** Same stale-Tag mechanism as claim 0. PickClasses takes the profile from _grid.Rows[rowIndex].Tag and the menu item's closure captures it. ChangeClass then calls SetRunsClass(profile, ...) and _profiles.Upsert(profile) without re-reading or merging. Phone garage paths replace the stored object and never redraw Who's Racing, so ticking a class reverts the phone's car pick and other phone-made fields.

**Fix** (`8678acd`). ChangeClass (CheckInControl.Classes.cs) now re-reads the driver with _profiles.Get(transponder) and changes the class on the copy stored now, not on the row's Tag. The undo's 'before' values also come from that stored copy, so a phone car pick made since the list was drawn is kept. If the driver has been removed in the meantime, it just redraws the list. Test: BugHunt19CheckInTests.Ticking_a_class_keeps_a_car_picked_on_the_phone_since_the_list_was_drawn.

### 3. Recording papers at the desk can quietly undo a driver's phone changes, such as the car they picked to race tonight.

`HeatCaller/HeatCaller/CheckInControl.Papers.cs:72` · medium · confirmed

**What happens.** 1. The Who's Racing list is drawn. 2. A driver picks 'racing tonight: Car B' on their phone. DriverRequests.RaceTonight saves a new snapshot with Upsert(after), and nothing redraws Who's Racing. 3. At the desk, someone clicks that driver's Papers cell, or uses Papers... / Papers for N Drivers... in the right-click menu, or Record Papers... in Desk mode, and saves the waiver. 4. The old profile object from the row, holding Car A, is written over the store. The phone's car pick is lost, and so are any of the driver's own phone edits the desk had approved. The same happens when a phone change lands while the modal papers window is open. This is the same overwrite bug hunt 18 fixed for DriverEditDialog and Import Entries, still present on these routes.

**Code path.** CheckInControl.Papers.cs:46-48 takes the profile from _grid.Rows[rowIndex].Tag. Lines 71-72 run form.WriteTo(profile) and then _profiles.Upsert(profile) with no ProfileStore.KeepChangesMadeSince. EditPapersForMany (114-118) does the same for every picked row's Tag. Desk mode's button (CheckInControl.Desk.cs:383) captures the profile when BuildNeeds runs, and that list is only rebuilt when the signature at 260-262 changes, so a car pick never refreshes it. ProfileStore.Upsert (ProfileStore.cs:748-760) replaces the dictionary entry with the object it is given. Phone writes store a new snapshot (DriverRequests.Garage.cs:602-631 and DriverRequests.cs:1427). No ProfileStore change event reaches CheckInControl: the only subscriber is MainForm.cs:741 CarsAdded, and GarageChanged only sets a dirty flag, so the row Tags stay stale.

**Verifier.** I traced it end to end. EditPapers and EditPapersForMany take the DriverProfile from the row's Tag, then call form.WriteTo/WriteToMany(profile) and _profiles.Upsert(profile) without going through KeepChangesMadeSince. DriverEditDialog, EntryImport and ProfileManagerControl all do use that merge. A phone car pick (RaceTonight, DriverRequests.Garage.cs:601-631) is applied at once with no desk approval: it saves a new snapshot through Upsert(after), and ProfileStore.Upsert swaps the dictionary entry to that new object. The only follow-up is GarageChangedByPhone, which goes to SetupLogChangedByPhone and GarageChanged (a dirty flag). Nothing calls CheckInControl.RefreshDriverList or RefreshAll. So the row Tag is still the old object with Car A, and saving papers writes it back over the store, losing the car pick. The modal window case works the same way.

**Fix** (`8678acd`). EditPapersFor re-reads the stored driver after the papers window closes with OK, then writes the papers onto it and saves. This covers the Papers cell, Desk mode's Record Papers and a phone change made while the window is open. EditPapersForMany does the same for each picked driver. Test: BugHunt19CheckInTests.Recording_papers_keeps_a_car_picked_on_the_phone_since_the_list_was_drawn, BugHunt19CheckInTests.Recording_papers_for_several_drivers_keeps_a_car_picked_on_the_phone.

### 4. The marshal no-show time penalty can miss the driver's next race and never be applied if that race is under another of their transponders

`HeatCaller/HeatCaller/MarshalRule.cs:168` · medium · confirmed

**What happens.** The club's rule is 'Time penalty in their next race'. A driver races Stock on transponder T1 and Mod on T2, where T2 is an alternate on the same profile. They race Mod Q1 H1 on T2, so MarshalAssigner gives them a post on the following heat under T2. They don't turn up and the desk logs the no-show, so it is stored with Transponder = T2. Their next race is Stock Q2 on T1. When it is saved, PendingPenalties builds `racing` from the raw result transponders ({T1, ...}) and asks racing.Contains(T2). That is false, so no penalty is added. The penalty only lands on their next Mod race, which is the wrong race. If they have no more Mod races tonight it never lands at all, because roundFilter is tonight's rounds only. Nothing tells the desk.

**Code path.** RaceControlControl.Marshals.cs:244 takes the transponder from MarshalPostsFor, the one the post is held under. MarshalAssigner.cs:73-74,185 builds posts from the previous heat's AssignedTransponders, which can be an alternate (MainForm.Marshals.cs:99-103 notes the post is 'held under the one they were gridded on in the heat before - their second car's, perhaps'). SetupStep8.cs:44 stores that transponder on the MarshalNoShow. MarshalRule.cs:159 builds the racing set from the raw r.Transponder, and line 168 compares it with x.NoShow.Transponder without resolving to the main transponder. ApplyPenalties at line 182 matches the entry by the raw transponder too. The other half of the rule does resolve it: QualifyingCalculator.cs:473 maps each forfeit through DriverKey(transponder, profiles), so the lose-a-qualifier rule handles alternates and the time-penalty rule does not.

**Verifier.** MarshalPostsFor (RaceControlControl.BetweenRaces.cs:392-414) returns p.Transponder, the transponder the driver was gridded on in that class's previous heat. MainForm.Marshals.cs:99-103 says outright that this can be a second car's. That value is passed to LogMarshalNoShow and stored on the MarshalNoShow. When a race is saved, RaceFilingService.cs:421 calls PendingPenalties, which builds the racing set from the raw result transponders (MarshalRule.cs:159) and compares them with NoShow.Transponder without resolving the profile's main transponder (line 168). ApplyPenalties at line 185 does the same. The documented rule is the driver's next race, but a next race under the other transponder gets no penalty. The penalty waits for a race on the same car, and if there is none tonight the round filter means it never lands. Nothing warns the desk. Only drivers with two transponders on one profile are affected.

**Fix** (`931d00e`). MarshalRule.PendingPenalties, ApplyPenalties and TakePenaltyOff now take an optional ProfileStore and match the driver by main transponder (PrimaryForAlternate, the same rule QualifyingCalculator uses). The entry match tries the exact transponder first. RaceFilingService and the undo in MainForm.SetupStep8 pass _profiles. Test: BugHunt19MarshalRuleTests.A_time_penalty_logged_on_the_second_cars_transponder_goes_on_the_next_race_on_the_first_car.

### 5. Editing and saving a night template that never set a heat size or round count saves 8 drivers per heat and 3 rounds, so later nights from that template stop using the club's own numbers.

`HeatCaller/HeatCaller/NightFields.cs:427` · medium · confirmed

**What happens.** A template was made from a day with DriversPerHeat=0 (or New Template on a club with nothing set), so the template has 0, meaning keep the last night's size. The club usually runs 6 per heat. They open Edit Template to change the entry fee and press Save. TemplateEditForm loads the scratch day showing 8, and NightFields.WriteTo writes day.DriversPerHeat = 8 with no 'shown, not written' guard. NightTemplates.From then copies 8, and the next night started from the template (ApplyTo, which only applies values above 0) builds heats of 8. QualifyingRounds 0 becomes 3 the same way, from the scratch RaceEvent default.

**Code path.** NightFields.cs:418-427 writes QualifyingRounds and DriversPerHeat unconditionally. Its Load comment (333-335) says heatSizeShown is 'shown, not written'. The Tonight host guards this with its baseline merge (RaceDayEventControl.cs:960-969), but TemplateEditForm.cs:78-80 loads `template.DriversPerHeat > 0 ? ... : 8` and Current() (222-224) calls _fields.WriteTo then NightTemplates.From with no guard. NightTemplates.cs:114-122 then applies the now non-zero values. RaceEvent.QualifyingRounds defaults to 3 (RaceEventStore.cs:102).

**Verifier.** RaceEvent.DriversPerHeat defaults to 0, and NightTemplates.From copies it. New Template uses From(LastRealDay()), so a template with 0 is common. TemplateEditForm loads it showing a hard-coded 8 (line 80; its own comment says '8 shown'). Current() then calls NightFields.WriteTo, which writes DriversPerHeat=(int)HeatSize.Value with no guard, then NightTemplates.From, so 8 is saved. ApplyTo applies values above 0, so nights started from that template build heats of 8 instead of the club's usual size (HeatSizeFor fallback). The Tonight host's baseline merge guards this; the template editor does not.

**Fix** (`a04c215`). TemplateEditForm now notes the rounds and heat size the fields opened with. In Current(), a new ShownNotWritten helper keeps a template's 0 (never set) unless the field was changed from that shown value, so saving no longer writes 8 per heat and 3 rounds. Test: BugHunt19_TemplatesTests.A_template_that_never_set_its_heat_size_or_rounds_keeps_them_unset_when_saved; BugHunt19_TemplatesTests.A_heat_size_or_rounds_changed_in_the_editor_is_saved.

### 6. If the OBS link drops while the stream is live, the stream piece stays open for good. A stop during the outage is never recorded, and a restarted stream never gets a piece of its own, so race video lines up against the wrong YouTube video.

`HeatCaller/HeatCaller/ObsLink.cs:163` · medium · confirmed

**What happens.** Video on, OBS streaming, and StreamTonight has an open piece. OBS crashes, or the network to the streaming PC drops, or HeatCaller is closed. The stream ends while the link is down, so OBS never sends the STOPPED event. HeatCaller reconnects, and GetStreamStatus answers outputActive=false, which is ignored. The piece keeps EndedUtc=null, Live stays true, and the relay document says every race since is in that video. If the club then starts streaming again (a new YouTube video), OBS_WEBSOCKET_OUTPUT_STARTED calls StreamTonight.Started, which returns early because a piece is still open. The second video never gets its own piece or link slot, and races after the restart are offset against the first piece's start.

**Code path.** ObsLink.cs:152-169: only an event, or op 7 with outputActive==true (164-166), touches _stream; outputActive false does nothing, and a dropped connection (catch at 101-115) never calls _stream.Stopped. StreamTonight.cs Started(): `if (_saved.Pieces.Count > 0 && _saved.Pieces[^1].EndedUtc is null) return;`. The class comment says 'A stream that drops and restarts is two pieces; each gets its own link'. The open piece also survives a HeatCaller restart, because it is loaded from stream-tonight.json.

**Verifier.** ObsLink.cs:152-169 changes _stream only on a STARTED/STOPPED event or when op 7 says outputActive==true. A dropped link goes to the catch in RunAsync and never calls Stopped. After reconnecting, outputActive=false is ignored, so the piece keeps EndedUtc=null. StreamTonight.Started returns early while the last piece is open, so a restarted stream never gets its own piece. That breaks the class's own 'drops and restarts is two pieces' rule.

LibVLC 3.0.24 (VideoLAN.LibVLC.Windows) - what HeatCaller ships, and under what licence
=======================================================================================

HeatCaller plays sponsor video slides with LibVLC and LibVLCSharp 3.10.1,
from VideoLAN. Both are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later; the text is LibVLC-LGPL-2.1.txt in this folder.

They ship unmodified, as separate DLLs in libvlc\win-x64 beside
HeatCaller.exe, so you can replace them with your own build of the same
version. Only the win-x64 copy is shipped, and only the plugins listed below:
HeatCaller.csproj leaves out whole categories it has no use for (streaming
out, muxing, network discovery, keystores, stream extractors,
visualisations, metadata, video splitting, Lua) and six single plugins
(SRT, RIST, DCP and Blu-ray access, and the x264 and AOM encoders).

from each DLL in the NuGet package on 15 Nov 2026. It is what the plugin's
authors declared. It has not been checked against each module's source
header, nor against every library a plugin has built into it. See the

NOTES AND OPEN QUESTIONS
------------------------

1. Two plugins declare the GPL, not the LGPL [CHECK]:
     audio_filter\libdolby_surround_decoder_plugin.dll
     audio_filter\libheadphone_channel_mixer_plugin.dll
   Both are small audio filters (Dolby Surround decoding, and a headphone
   downmix effect). HeatCaller never asks for either, but VLC can pick a

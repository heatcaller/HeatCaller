Live on a phone · plan item 4

Your own card at the top, a four-column board under it, and your row kept in view. Seven states of the same Live tab, each on a 360 px phone (the narrowest common Android width). Tap any board row to open it in place; scroll the board in frame 2 to see your row stay pinned.

Colours come from the club's look tokens and the Pit Wall kit. The switch shows the club's dark look, a club light look, and item 6's high contrast in dark and light.

How it's built

Board columnsUnder 480 px wide: POS · DRIVER · GAP · LAST (34 / flexible / 70 / 62 px). GAP shows the club's own pick (Gap to P1, Gap behind or Interval), named in the header chip. A lapped car shows +1L. Wider screens keep today's full table.
You-cardShows for the signed-in driver (or the followed one, with a ☆ "Following" label instead of "You") when they're in the running race. Car ahead and behind come from the standings order. Leading: "Leading" with nobody ahead. Last: "Last on the road" with nobody behind. Not in this race: their next race and its time from the programme.
Gap lineThe car ahead, you and the car behind, spaced by their gaps on a scale of the larger gap × 1.2 (so both fit). Over 5 s or a lap, the dot sits at the end and the label says so.
Your rowposition: sticky; top: 0; bottom: 0 inside the board, so it sticks to the edge it's scrolling off. The tonal fill and primary edge are the same as the you-card, so the eye links them.
Open a rowTap a row for Best, Laps and Trend in place (and Gap to P1 or Gap ahead, whichever the column isn't showing). One open at a time. aria-expanded on the row. Tap again to close.
All columns"All columns" under the board opens a sheet with today's seven-column table. Position and driver stay put while the rest scroll sideways. Portrait only (the 7 Nov pick).
FreshnessEvery number shown is from a frame under 2 s old, or the whole card dims with item 1's strip ("No signal. Showing lap 8 from 14 s ago."). Gaps are never shown as live from an old frame (the trust-over-chatter rule).
Screen readersOne aria-live="polite" line under the you-card, at most every 10 s and only on a change: "You're third, 0.42 behind Al Gorithim." The board is a list of rows with a header row, each row a button.
Text size and zoomEverything in rem, so item 6's A+ / A++ and the phone's own text size grow it. Checked at 130%: names truncate with an ellipsis, numbers never wrap. Pinch-zoom works (item 6).
Fonts and coloursNumbers in Barlow Condensed (the Live flag bar's font), tabular figures so the columns don't jump. Gap ahead in --warn, gap behind in --good, personal-best laps in --accent. No hex outside the look tokens.

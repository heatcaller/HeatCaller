Bigger pack, transponder and setup buttons

Today the three ways to fix what the hero card says are 34px chips with a 7px dot. Each option below turns them into real buttons at least 52px tall, in the Pit Wall kit and the club's own colours. The rest of the card stays as it is. Every phone works: tap one that is waiting and it turns ready, and tap the pack once it's set to swap to the next pack.

What they do (unchanged)

  • Pack opens the quick pack swap sheet
  • Transponder starts the check on Tonight
  • Setup opens Log setup in the Garage. It only shows when there's a car tonight

Rules every option keeps

  • Every tap target 52px tall or more. Today's chips are 34px
  • Ready is green with a tick, waiting is amber. Colour is never the only sign: there's a word and an icon too
  • Colours come from look tokens only, so club looks and light mode still apply

Try

  • Switch between Before the race and All ready at the top
  • Untick Car tonight to see two buttons instead of three
  • The grey pill under each name is the measured tap height
State
Built against meMoment() in relay/src/mespaces.ts, where the three meChip calls sit in the "Up next" branch. Whichever you pick replaces the .row of chips only. The click handlers (mePackSheet, meToTransponder, meLogSetup) stay as they are.

Relay · Home tab · 11 Nov 2026

Home links become Pit Wall buttons

The four accent-coloured text links on Home turn into the kit's buttons: full-width, 44px tall, with soft 14px corners and the cyan focus ring, coloured only from the club's look. They're built as outline buttons, the same as "Lap records and the quickest drivers" under Our tracks. Use the bar below to try tonal or primary instead.

What changes
  • a.more becomes a.page.wide.hp-go
  • The two links inside lists move out under the list
  • The trailing → goes, because the button shape already says "tap me"
What stays
  • Same words and the same destinations
  • Cards and their order on Home
  • Other tabs' "more" links (Me, check-in)
Kit rule
  • Outline = neutral onward step
  • Primary is kept for the one main action on a screen, and Home has none
  • No hex colours outside the look tokens

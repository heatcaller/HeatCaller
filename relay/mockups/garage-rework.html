Garage rework: three options

The Garage space on Me, three ways. Every phone works: swipe the cars, tap Manage in A, the tabs in B and the car chips in C. The cars, packs and numbers are made up. The look is the Pit Wall kit on the Synthwave club look.

Today
  • The carousel shows only with 2+ cars, inside the "My garage" card
  • The setup sheet has its own car dropdown, so it can show a different car from the carousel
  • Four buttons on every car, and Retire is as easy to hit as Edit
  • One pack picker per race, and a 7-column pack table
True in all three
  • Only races with no pack yet are asked about ("3 races need a pack")
  • Pack table cut to Pack · Median · Fade · Gap
  • Retire and Remove sit behind Manage
  • No changes to the data, the laptop or the contract

# Bug hunt 14 (run 7 Nov 2026)

Base: `main` at `f427f69` (PRs #100 and #101: the AI-written achievements
read, and the Desk button on Race Control's flag row). Branch `bughunt-14`.
Full C# suite 6,010/6,010 passed and relay 1,527/1,527 passed on 8 Nov 2026.

## How it was run

- **Nine finders**, one per area: race control and timing, the after-race
  recap and commentary, achievements and the new AI read, the Desk button
  and flag row, championships, voice and the announcer, cast / Big Screen /
  On Air, the LAN web server, and the relay. Each was told to trace every
  finding end to end and drop anything a test or comment pins as deliberate.
- **Skeptics** checked every finding independently before anything was
  fixed. 25 findings were reported; all 25 held up, three only in part (H2,
  L2, B3 - the parts that held are what was fixed).
- **Fixers** on disjoint sets of files, each with a regression test per
  fix, then **one reviewer** over the whole diff. It found five problems in
  the fixes, all put right before the full suite ran (see the end).

## Fixed

### Race control and timing
- **T1** A black-flagged car at the lap limit still brought the checkered
  out on the green after a red, or when the desk gave it +1 lap. Bug hunt 13
  only covered the crossing that reaches the limit.
- **T2** A yellow or red thrown after the finish (F4/F5 or the phone) stalled
  the next automatic start begun with Countdown ("the yellow flag is out").
  A fresh countdown now clears a leftover caution, keeping a checkered.

### Desk button
- **D1** Desk did nothing visible when Driver Requests was minimised:
  `Activate()` doesn't restore a minimised window. Online Sign-ups had the
  same gap.
- **D2** Desk coming or going re-centred the row under a held Red,
  Checkered or Reset, which called the hold off. The re-layout now waits for
  the let-go.

### After the flag and the achievements read
- **R1** A recap written but never spoken (teaser off, or called off before
  it played) stayed on the Announcer page as the "last race summary". The
  log also said it was called off "before it was written".
- **R2** The unofficial result at the flag named a disqualified or no-lap car
  among those beaten - or as the winner of a one-car race. Same filter now
  for the flag hand-off and the built-in flag throw; a DQ's fastest lap is
  not credited.
- **H1** The who-got-what check couldn't see a driver called by first name,
  so "…and Billy matches her with Personal Best" credited Jane and passed.
  First names now count (capitalised, unique, not opening a sentence).
- **H2** When the AI read was written but not voiced in time, the built-in
  read went out but the reader's hand back still reacted to the AI read.
- **H3** "Earned three achievements in this race" used the read's cap of
  three, not what the driver earned.
- **H4** The number check only saw digits, so "her eighth achievement" or
  "the first at the club" passed when the facts said otherwise; numbers from
  earlier reads also passed. Number words and club-first claims are now
  checked against this race's badge facts.

### Championships
- **C1** Public standings "What it takes" counted dropped rounds twice, so
  it said every round was run and could crown a champion early. It now
  counts race days, and says nothing when races can't be put on days.
- **C2** A DNF that kept its laps (and a DQ) was described to the AI as
  "entered without completing a lap" - now "without a finish".
- **C3** Recap and intro facts called races "rounds" ("9 rounds counted"
  after 6 nights) - now "races counted".

### Voice
- **V1** Reset left a position report waiting, and it was read out up to a
  minute after the reset, from the abandoned race.
- **V2** "One minute to go" had no say-by time, so a busy speaker could say
  it with 20 seconds left. Now 15 s.

### Cast and On Air
- **B1** Holding 0 to skip On Air staged the race and then wiped its own
  "drivers to the line" call: `Cancel()` ran the hand-over inline before the
  cut. Both skip sites now use `CancelAsync()`.
- **B2** A sponsor video with sound kept playing over the On Air jingle and
  opening. On Air takes the spot down before the jingle.
- **B3** Replay Moment pressed again mid-race replayed the last replay. The
  replay ring starts again after a replay while the race is recording.

### LAN server
- **W1** A phone check-in on a closed race day answered "checked in" while
  nothing changed. The host now tells the server the day is closed, and the
  phone gets the reason.
- **W2** /vote said "Voting has closed." when no vote had been opened.
- **W3** The Room & Web card (and the docs) said a vote closes when the
  result comes in; it closes at the green.

### Relay (needs `fly deploy` from relay/ and an app restart)
- **L1** Sponsor "seen" and tap counts were capped at 20 per 5 minutes per
  address - a whole venue's Wi-Fi - and a refused view was never retried.
  Now a flood brake of 300; a view is stamped only once the relay answers,
  busy answers retry after a minute, and refusals are final.
- **L2** The relay room vote counted a venue's Wi-Fi as one voter (and the
  7th phone got "changed your mind"). Each phone now sends a random id,
  hashed with the address and the ballot's salt; one address is capped at 40
  voters per ballot, and the brake is 100 sends per 5 minutes with its own
  wording.

## Caught by the review of the fixes
- The H4 word check turned down accurate reads: badge descriptions use
  "second" and "third" themselves, and details give counts as digits.
- Sponsor views retried on every scroll with no backoff, even after a
  permanent refusal.
- A log line lost its space ("Recap queued3.2s").
- A first name opening a sentence ("Will she stop there?") counted as that
  driver.
- The vote brake's 429 still said "you have changed your mind".

## Left as it is
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Desk restores a maximised-then-minimised Driver Requests window un-maximised | Same as every other "bring it forward" window in the app (Mission Control, Broadcast) |
| The AI hand back is only length-checked, and written from the first 200 characters of the read | Old behaviour, not new in #100; a short reaction line |
| Moving the Desk on a wide row still shifts the racing buttons along | Asked for ("adjust the spacing"); only holds are protected |
| A phone that votes on an old cached page and then a new one in the same race counts twice | Only across a refresh mid-race |
| "Release X now" from a stale menu can move a released car's zero | Only reachable from a menu left open |
